# DEEN AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

DEEN AI is an educational Islamic learning application.

## Information sent to the AI service

When a user submits a question in the AI chat, the text of that question may be sent to the DEEN AI backend so that an AI response can be generated.

The app should not request passwords, payment card numbers, authentication codes, or other sensitive personal information.

## Service providers

The backend may use an AI service provider to process submitted questions and generate responses. The server is designed so that the provider credential remains on the server and is not embedded in the mobile application.

## Data retention

The initial app does not intentionally create a user account or maintain a personal profile. Any server or provider logs should be configured according to the final hosting and AI-service settings before public release.

## Children's privacy

DEEN AI is intended to provide educational content. The final public release must use age-appropriate privacy and safety settings and comply with applicable laws and Google Play requirements.
